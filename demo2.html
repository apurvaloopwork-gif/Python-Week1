DSA Agent

An AI-powered Direct Selling Agent for loan origination. It identifies borrowers, pre-qualifies profiles, routes applicants to the right NBFC product, follows up on WhatsApp and calls, and hands off to a human only at the final stage.

A consent-based income verification tool for gig workers. It uses browser automation to capture earnings history from platforms and generates a standardized income certificate for NBFC underwriting.

Market: 15M+ Indian gig workers Model: ₹50-150 per verification Focus: DPDP consent and no credential retention
